# LeadFlow screenshot use cases

These captures document working user flows from the local NEXORA LeadFlow dashboard. All leads and contact details shown are synthetic demo data using reserved `.example` email domains. Captures were made with `MOCK_AI=true`; HubSpot, Slack, and Resend were disabled, and no email was approved or sent.

| Artifact | Use case |
| --- | --- |
| `dashboard-overview.jpg` | Review lead volume, HOT/WARM/NURTURE counts, required human reviews, integration modes, and the recent lead queue. |
| `lead-detail-hot.jpg` | Inspect deterministic HOT qualification, analysis confidence, fit signals, and recommended sales action. |
| `lead-detail-review.jpg` | See a NURTURE lead routed to human review because company, industry, budget, and timeline are missing. |
| `approval-workflow.jpg` | Review the response draft and the explicit human approval control; status remains **Not sent**. |
| `leadflow-demo.gif` | Animated walkthrough of the dashboard, HOT analysis, missing-information review, and approval-gated draft. |

The GIF is a short sequence of the saved screenshots, not a continuous screen recording.
